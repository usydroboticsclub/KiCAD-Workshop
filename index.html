Design your first PCB in KiCad

Welcome! This is a step-by-step workshop guide to take you through the basics of KiCad. We’ll be working on a flashing PCB Keychain in the shape of the USRC Logo, and you’ll be taken through the entire PCB designing process from making your schematic, assigning and making your own symbols and footprints, laying your board out, and more!

Start Workshop

Disclaimer: This workshop was created using KiCad 10.0 on Windows. The general steps should be similar on other operating systems, but some visuals and interface details may differ.

Workshop Path

Work through each section

01

Introduction

What are printed circuit boards (PCBs), why do we need them, and what are the tools we use to design them?

 02

Getting Familiar with the Circuit

Explore the circuit you'll build in this workshop and see how its components work together.

 03

Creating a Project

An overview of KiCad's features, plus getting started with your project.

 04

Building the Schematic

Using the Schematic Editor to place circuit symbols and begin drawing your design in KiCad.

 05

Assigning Footprints

Choosing schematic symbols for your circuit and assigning footprints that match the physical components.

 06

Custom Symbols & Footprints

Building project library symbols and footprints for components that aren't included in KiCad's libraries.

 07

PCB Layout, Layers and Outline

Bring your schematic into the PCB Editor, define the board outline, and work with copper layers and exposed copper features.

 08

Placement, Routing and Board Validation

Arrange and route the board, check design rules, and inspect the PCB in 3D.

 09

Manufacturing

Export Gerber and drill files, then package them for PCB fabrication.

 10

Design Submission

Gather your completed project and output files, then submit your design here!

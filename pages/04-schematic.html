Written Guide Step-by-Step: Build the Schematic
01

Open Schematic Editor

From the project manager, launch the Schematic Editor.

Add screenshot: Schematic Editor button
02

Place circuit symbols

Hover over the canvas and press A to open the Symbol Chooser. Search for a component from your circuit, select the matching symbol, and place it on the sheet.

Add screenshot: Symbol Chooser window
✓ Checkpoint: The symbols you need are placed on the schematic sheet.
03

Connect the circuit

Use the wire tool to connect symbol pins according to the circuit you explored in the previous lesson. Keep connections clear and save the project as you work.

✓ Checkpoint: Your schematic shows the circuit's components and their intended connections.
Lesson checkpoint: Your schematic includes the circuit symbols and the connections between them.

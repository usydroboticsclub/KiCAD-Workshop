Written Guide Step-by-Step: Place, Route and Validate the Board
01

Review component placement

Check component orientation, spacing, connector access, and room for clean routing before finalising the layout.

Add screenshot: Component placement review
02

Route the connections

Follow the ratsnest lines to connect the component pads. Hover over a pad and press X to start a track, then choose the appropriate copper layer when a connection needs to change layers.

Add screenshot: Routed PCB tracks
✓ Checkpoint: The ratsnest lines disappear as their connections are routed.
03

Run the Design Rules Checker

Run DRC after routing to check clearances, board constraints, and unconnected items. Review each reported issue, correct the ones that apply, and rerun the check to confirm the board is ready for final review.

Add screenshot: DRC results window
Review every result. Don't dismiss errors or warnings until you understand whether they need to be fixed.
04

Preview the board in 3D

Open KiCad's 3D Viewer to inspect component orientation, overall placement, and the finished board shape.

Add screenshot: 3D Viewer render
Lesson checkpoint: Components are arranged, connections are routed, DRC results are reviewed, and the board has been inspected in 3D.
